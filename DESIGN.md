# Research workspace design

Mode: Operate / Read. User-approved direction: light professional research workspace.

Use a pale blue-grey canvas, white navigation rail, deep navy typography and a restrained teal primary series. Amber identifies hypothetical or cautionary information. Preserve metric meanings and data provenance. Charts, not decoration, lead the analytical views.

Five primary destinations: Overview, Data explorer, Model validation, Decision lab, Research. Secondary controls remain visible within each destination. Forms group scenario assumptions; captions identify cohort and methodology. Typography uses a clean sans serif with sentence-case headings, compact tables and generous separation between analytical sections. Use native responsive columns; avoid decorative gradients, emoji and heavy borders.

Palette: canvas #F5F7FB, surface #FFFFFF, foreground #172B4D, accent #167D8D, comparator series #64748B, scenario #B7791F. Graphs use consistent English labels and percent formatting.
