# Product context

Credit Risk Decision Lab is a student fintech research portfolio for credit-risk internships. Its audience is the learner and interviewers assessing evidence and reasoning. The current user explicitly requested a light, professional research workspace with English-only product content and useful sub-options.

Primary tasks: inspect recorded model results, explore data, study limited review capacity and hypothetical costs, inspect public historical examples, and read/download the report. Claims must distinguish historical observations, model estimates and scenario assumptions. Preserve the fixed model, splits and evaluation limitations. New test analyses are post-evaluation diagnostics, not untouched confirmation.

Local Windows access is required. Public hosting has not been configured. Native Streamlit controls and charts should remain usable on narrower screens.
