**Credit Risk Decision Lab** | Python, Pandas, scikit-learn, Streamlit

- Analysed 30,000 historical credit-card records and built a reproducible train/validation/test workflow for next-month default modelling.
- Evaluated a random-forest model on 6,000 held-out records, recording ROC-AUC of 0.774, average precision of 0.550 and Brier score of 0.136; documented prior full-sample exploration and the absence of external validation.
- Examined threshold trade-offs and review-capacity scenarios; the top 10% of test records by predicted risk contained 415 historical defaults among 600 review slots.
- Built a local interactive research dashboard for model evaluation, hypothetical cost scenarios and synthetic probability demonstrations.
